Privacy

No tracking, no ads, no analytics. What you make stays on your computer unless you publish a skin.

Your game files

When you connect Assets.zip your browser reads the character files out of it and keeps a copy on your device, so you only do it once. They're never uploaded and the site doesn't host any Hytale files. You can remove the copy any time with "Forget game files" in Help.

Your skins

Skins are saved in your browser. If you pick a skins folder in the Locker they're also written there as small files. They aren't sent to us or anyone else.

Signing in and publishing

Sign in with Hytale isn't switched on yet, this is how it will work. Signing in is optional and only needed to publish. You sign in on Hytale's own page so we never see your password. Our server swaps the sign-in code with Hytale once, reads the profile you pick and drops the tokens straight away. One cookie called __Host-se_session keeps you signed in for 30 days and isn't used for anything else. Signing out ends it on every device.

If Hytale tells us which paid items your account owns, that list goes to your browser so you can edit those items, and it stays there. Our server doesn't keep it.

What our server keeps

Only if you sign in and publish. Your profile's UUID and username, the skins you publish with their names and the list of game items they use, and a count of your recent changes that's gone after an hour and only there to stop spam. When anyone signs in we also keep a scrambled one-way code made from their IP address for 10 minutes, so nobody can hammer the sign-in. The address itself is never stored. Custom pieces are never uploaded. Everything is stored with Cloudflare, who host the site, and isn't sold or shared with anyone else.

It's kept until you remove it. You can unpublish any skin whenever you want, and "Delete my published data" in the Locker removes everything we have on you straight away.

Other services

Pressing Find in Players sends the username you typed to playerdb.co to get that player's skin, nothing else. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which sees normal request data like your IP to serve pages and block attacks, we don't use it. Bug reports go to GitHub and are public, so don't put personal stuff in them. Tips go through Ko-fi under their own privacy terms, and we don't get or keep anything from them.

Kids

The editor itself needs no personal info at all. Publishing needs a Hytale account, so it follows Hytale's own age rules for accounts.

Your rights

You can see and delete everything we hold about you from the Locker at any time. Wherever you live, if you want a copy of it or have any other privacy question, ask on the project's GitHub.

Who runs this

Skin Editor is a one-person fan project by King-Talon23, who decides what's kept and why. If this page changes the date below changes too.
