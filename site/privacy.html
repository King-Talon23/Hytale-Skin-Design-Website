Privacy

Short version: the Skin Editor has no accounts, no tracking and no server of its own. What you make stays on your computer.

What we collect

Nothing. The site has no sign-up, sets no cookies, runs no analytics and shows no ads. We have no database, so there is nothing of yours for us to hold, sell or lose.

Your game files

When you connect Assets.zip, your browser reads the character files out of it and keeps a copy in its own storage on your device, so you only connect once. Those files are never uploaded. The website hosts no Hytale files. You can remove the copy at any time from Help, with "Forget game files".

Your skins

Skins are saved in your browser's storage. If you choose a skins folder in the Wardrobe, they are also written there as small files, and only in the folder you picked. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Player lookup

When you press Find in the Players tab, the username you typed is sent to playerdb.co, a public community service, to fetch that player's currently equipped skin. Nothing else is sent, and it only happens when you press Find. That service has its own privacy terms.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, it handles technical request data such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks. We do not use that data and do not run analytics on it.

Feedback

Bug reports and feature requests are posted on GitHub and are public. GitHub's own privacy terms apply there. Please do not include personal details or game files in a report.

Sign in with Hytale (planned)

Signing in with a Hytale account is not available on this site yet. If it is added, the site would receive only what you approve on Hytale's own consent screen: an anonymous identifier and, if you allow it, the game profile you select (its UUID and username). It would be kept in your browser and used to show your own skins. We would never see your password, and we would not store your details on a server or share them.

Children

The site collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
