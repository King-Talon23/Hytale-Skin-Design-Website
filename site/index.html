Build outfits from every cosmetic in your game, keep 45 extra skins, and preview them with the game's own animations. The editor reads the cosmetics from your own copy of Hytale, so connect it once to begin.

The editor copies the small character files it needs (about 27 MB of the 4 GB) into this browser, so you only do this once per game update.
