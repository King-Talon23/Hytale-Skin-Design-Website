Terms

Short and plain. Using the site means you're fine with these.

What this is

Skin Editor is a free, unofficial fan tool. It isn't made, approved or endorsed by Hypixel Studios, and Hytale is their trademark. The site doesn't host or hand out any Hytale files. It only reads the game files you already have, in your own browser.

Using it

Use it to design skins for yourself. Don't use it to copy and pass around the game's files, and don't publish skins with names that are hateful, sexual, harassing or pretending to be someone else. Paid and edition items stay locked unless Hytale says your account owns them.

Published skins

If you publish a skin anyone can see it under your username. You can unpublish it whenever you want. Skins or names that break the rules above can be hidden, and profiles that keep doing it can be blocked from publishing. To report something, open an issue on the project's GitHub.

No promises

The site is provided as it is, for free. It can change, break or go away, so keep a backup of anything you care about, there's one in Help. We aren't responsible for what you do with skins you make here.

Privacy

What the site keeps and how to delete it is on the privacy page.
